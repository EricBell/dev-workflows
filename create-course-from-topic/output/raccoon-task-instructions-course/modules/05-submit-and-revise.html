Submit and revise: feedback is part of the pipeline

Treat submission like sending a draft to an editor, not dropping a bottle into the ocean.

Feedback requests use a four-part shape

1

What you are working on

Repo plus the failure or signal you found.

2

What feedback you want

Prompt phrasing, grader structure, calibration, difficulty, or another specific question.

3

What is missing

Tell reviewers which pieces are incomplete so they do not waste attention.

4

WIP status note

For drafts, say what is clearly WIP, known-bad, or closer to done.
